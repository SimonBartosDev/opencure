# Leishmaniasis — outreach brief

> **Disease class:** `parasitic` &nbsp; | &nbsp; **Lead disease:** no

## Why this disease

Leishmania spp. (visceral, cutaneous). Pentamidine, sodium stibogluconate, miltefosine — toxic, resistance widespread.

3. **3,4-Dihydroxycinnamic Acid**  (p = 0.00) — Curcumin

## Suggested assay

- **Assay:** Intracellular amastigote screen (THP-1 infected with L. donovani)
- **Readout:** Parasite count at 72 h; selectivity vs host viability
