# Niemann-Pick disease — outreach brief

> **Disease class:** `rare_metabolic` &nbsp; | &nbsp; **Lead disease:** **yes**

## Why this disease

Lysosomal storage disorder caused by NPC1/NPC2 mutations (Niemann-Pick C) or SMPD1 (Niemann-Pick A/B). Cholesterol trafficking defect, neurodegeneration, hepatosplenomegaly. Miglustat slows disease but is not curative. Pediatric onset, fatal.
