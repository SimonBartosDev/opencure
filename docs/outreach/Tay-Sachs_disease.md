# Tay-Sachs disease — outreach brief

> **Disease class:** `rare_metabolic` &nbsp; | &nbsp; **Lead disease:** no

## Why this disease

HEXA deficiency. GM2 ganglioside accumulation. No approved therapy; substrate-reduction therapy in trials.
