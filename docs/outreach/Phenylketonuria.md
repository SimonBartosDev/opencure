# Phenylketonuria — outreach brief

> **Disease class:** `rare_metabolic` &nbsp; | &nbsp; **Lead disease:** no

## Why this disease

PAH deficiency. Sapropterin, pegvaliase. Lifelong dietary restriction.

## Suggested assay

- **Assay:** PAH activity rescue / Phe clearance in patient hepatocytes (iPSC-derived)
- **Readout:** Phe → Tyr conversion at 72 h
