# Chagas disease — outreach brief

> **Disease class:** `parasitic` &nbsp; | &nbsp; **Lead disease:** **yes**

## Why this disease

Trypanosoma cruzi infection; cardiomyopathy in 30 % of chronic cases. Benznidazole and nifurtimox are toxic and only 30-60 % curative. ~7 M infected, largely Latin America.
