# Acromegaly — outreach brief

> **Disease class:** `rare_metabolic` &nbsp; | &nbsp; **Lead disease:** no

## Why this disease

Growth-hormone hypersecretion (pituitary adenoma). Somatostatin analogues, pegvisomant. Treatment-resistant cases unmet.
