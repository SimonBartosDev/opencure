# Leprosy — outreach brief

> **Disease class:** `bacterial` &nbsp; | &nbsp; **Lead disease:** no

## Why this disease

M. leprae. WHO MDT (rifampicin + clofazimine + dapsone) curative; nerve damage often irreversible by diagnosis.

## Suggested assay

- **Assay:** MIC against M. leprae (mouse footpad model is gold-standard but slow; cell culture surrogate via M. ulcerans / M. smegmatis)
- **Readout:** Growth inhibition at 14 d
