# Sickle cell disease — outreach brief

> **Disease class:** `rare_metabolic` &nbsp; | &nbsp; **Lead disease:** **yes**

## Why this disease

Hemoglobin S polymerisation under deoxygenation, RBC sickling, vaso-occlusion. Hydroxyurea, voxelotor, crizanlizumab approved; still high unmet need (acute pain crises, cumulative organ damage). ~100 K affected in the US, ~5 M worldwide.

## Suggested assay

- **Assay:** Anti-sickling assay on SS RBCs ex vivo, deoxygenation challenge
- **Readout:** Sickling rate by morphometry; HbF induction (qPCR)
- **Ask (compound supply):** 30 µL at 0.1 / 1 / 10 µM
