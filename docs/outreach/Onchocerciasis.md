# Onchocerciasis — outreach brief

> **Disease class:** `parasitic` &nbsp; | &nbsp; **Lead disease:** no

## Why this disease

Onchocerca volvulus, river blindness. Ivermectin MDA + Wolbachia targeting (doxycycline).

- **Readout:** Motility / WST-1 over 72 h
