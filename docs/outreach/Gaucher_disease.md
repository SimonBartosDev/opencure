# Gaucher disease — outreach brief

> **Disease class:** `rare_metabolic` &nbsp; | &nbsp; **Lead disease:** no

## Why this disease

GBA1 deficiency; glucocerebroside accumulation. Enzyme replacement (imiglucerase) standard. Type 2/3 neurological forms unmet.
