# Fabry disease — outreach brief

> **Disease class:** `rare_metabolic` &nbsp; | &nbsp; **Lead disease:** no

## Why this disease

GLA deficiency; α-galactosidase A. Enzyme replacement + chaperone (migalastat) standard. Cardiac variant unmet.
