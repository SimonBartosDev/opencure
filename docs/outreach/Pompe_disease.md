# Pompe disease — outreach brief

> **Disease class:** `rare_metabolic` &nbsp; | &nbsp; **Lead disease:** no

## Why this disease

GAA deficiency. Glycogen accumulation in lysosomes. Alglucosidase alfa standard; muscle penetration limited.
