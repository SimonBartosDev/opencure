# Spinocerebellar ataxia — outreach brief

> **Disease class:** `chronic_systemic` &nbsp; | &nbsp; **Lead disease:** no

## Why this disease

Heterogeneous (SCA1-50). Polyglutamine and noncoding-repeat expansions. No disease-modifying therapy.
