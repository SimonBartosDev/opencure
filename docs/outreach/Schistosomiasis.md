# Schistosomiasis — outreach brief

> **Disease class:** `parasitic` &nbsp; | &nbsp; **Lead disease:** **yes**

## Why this disease

Schistosoma mansoni / haematobium / japonicum. Adult worms in mesenteric and urogenital venous plexus. ~200 M infected globally; praziquantel is the only WHO-recommended drug, resistance is emerging.

## Suggested assay

- **Assay:** Adult-worm viability assay (S. mansoni NMRI strain), 48-72 h
- **Readout:** WST-1 reduction or direct microscopic motility
- **Ask (compound supply):** 20-30 µL of purchased compound at 1, 10, 100 µM
