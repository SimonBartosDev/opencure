# OpenCure Lab Outreach Briefs (NTDs + rare diseases)

One-page partnership briefs for each of the 22 NTDs and 19 rare diseases the v7 platform screens. Pair each brief with the live dashboard URL and the content-fingerprinted Zenodo snapshot. Customize per-PI when reaching out.

Lead diseases (deepest curation, highest-priority outreach):
**Schistosomiasis · Chagas disease · Sickle cell disease · Niemann-Pick disease**.

Per-disease briefs live under `docs/outreach/<disease_key>.md`.
